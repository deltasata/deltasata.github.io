EO-Agent · ask it yourself

Ask the Earth a question. Get an answer you can check.

Was there a flood here? Did this forest burn? Is something being built? EO-Agent reads the question, fetches every free Sentinel-2 image of the place before and after, masks the clouds, measures the change with trained models and answers yes, no, or "cannot tell", with the images as evidence.

1 · Ask it yourself

Pick a place and an event, or type a question. It runs on our cluster, one question at a time. A place nobody has asked about takes 1 to 5 minutes, mostly to download and cloud-mask the images.
