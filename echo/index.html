The telescope

How the blend is observed.

Signal: damped random walk (τ ≈ 316 d), the standard model for quasar variability. Trial ratio μ fixed at 0.3. With noise, the blend is pre-smoothed at three scales and the curves are combined, as in the paper.

The statistics, in four lines

Model. Only the sum is observed:

F(t) = f(t) + μ̂ · f(t − Δt̂) f: unknown random signal, μ̂ ≤ 1, Δt̂: delay

Degeneracy. For any trial (μ, Δt) the blend inverts exactly, so every guess fits the data perfectly:

f_rec(t) = Σₙ (−μ)ⁿ F(t − nΔt)

The statistic. Score each guess by the roughness of its rebuild, then standardise across guesses:

ε(Δt) = Σᵢ [f_rec(tᵢ₊₁) − f_rec(tᵢ)]² Σ(Δt) = (ε − ⟨ε⟩) / σ_ε

Why it works (the proof). With H the difference series of F, ε expands as a power series in μ whose leading term is the autocorrelation of the derivative. Differencing whitens the slowly varying signal, so the echo shows up as sharp dips at ±Δt̂, and the odd higher-order terms deepen them further:

ε(Δt) ≈ ε₀ [1 − 2μ · ACF(H; Δt)] + O(μ²) ε(0) = ε₀ / (1+μ)²

Toggle the two autocorrelation overlays above to see it: the derivative ACF tracks Σ, while the raw-blend ACF is swamped by the signal's long memory.

Method: S. Bag, A. Shafieloo, K. Liao & T. Treu, ApJ 927, 191 (2022) · S. Bag, W. Sohn, A. Shafieloo & K. Liao, ApJ 950, 158 (2023). Simulated data, computed live in your browser.
